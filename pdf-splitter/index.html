PDF Splitter

Cut big PDFs into parts that fit a Claude Project. Nothing leaves this device.

Each part is sized so Claude reads all of it: up to 100 pages and under 30 MB.

How to use: pick your PDFs, tap Save all, then upload every part and the index file to your Claude Project.

Claude reads a PDF of 100 pages or fewer as text and as page images, so charts, tables and scans all come through. Parts end where a chapter starts if the PDF has bookmarks. Each page is stamped with its page number in the original, so the pages Claude cites match your copy.
